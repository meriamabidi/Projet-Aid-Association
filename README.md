**🌟 Smart Aid Association Desktop Management Application 🌟**
**📜 Overview**
The Smart Aid Association Desktop Management Application is a comprehensive software solution designed to streamline the management processes of the Smart Aid Association. This application enhances administrative management and smart aid distribution for individuals with disabilities. It simplifies tasks and digitalizes processes, saving valuable time and resources.

**🛠️ Functional Requirements**
**🎯 Activity Management:**
Manage and organize activities for individuals with disabilities, including scheduling and tracking.
**♿ Disability Management:** Add and manage the data of people with disabilities, including personal information and specific needs, to better assist them with appropriate resources and aid.
**🎁 Donation Management:** Track and manage donations for the association, ensuring transparency and efficient use of resources.
**💸 Expense Management:** Monitor and manage expenses to maintain the financial health of the association.
**👩‍💼 Employee Management:** Manage staff, their roles, and responsibilities within the organization.
**💻 Technical Tools**
**Programming Language:** C++ (Object-Oriented Programming) 💡
**IDE:** Qt Creator 🖥️
**Graphical Framework:** Qt 🎨
**Database Management:**
Power AMC for modeling 🧑‍💻
Oracle for database storage 💾
**Electronics Integration:** Arduino for hardware integration 🛠️
**Version Control:** Git/GitHub for collaborative development and version control 🔄
